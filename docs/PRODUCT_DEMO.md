# Growth decision loop — demonstration guide

Use your existing manually imported posts, or explicitly labeled test/demo data. Never present fixtures as real X discussions or outcomes. No paid API access is necessary. Keep Live disabled.

1. **Sources & Settings**: verify at least five configurable accounts and review the audience/positioning/keywords. The initial brief is provisional; do not claim unverified TelyClaw capabilities.
2. **Discover / Understand**: choose Manual Imports and 7D, then rerun Analyze. Try 24H or 4H to demonstrate publication-time filtering. Show cluster names, deduplicated post/author counts, summary excerpts, all source text/links/times/metrics and partial engagement. Explain that the method can still split or merge imperfectly.
3. **Prioritize**: inspect up to three relevant opportunities. Read Why It's Trending, Why TelyClaw Should Care and Recommended Content Move. Show effective score weights. With insufficient historical coverage, say “Insufficient historical data”—never invent momentum.
4. **Create**: generate each selected trend's three source-conditioned angles and a suggested X post. State whether it is local template or optional LLM output. Add your expertise; keep it within the displayed conservative 280-character ASCII/no-URL policy.
5. **Approve**: save/edit and approve. Editing again hides publication actions and revokes approval on save. TXT export still downloads approved text.
6. **Publish**: Open in X only opens a prefilled composer. Publication on X requires your own deliberate action. If you do not want to actually publish during an interview, stop here and explain the remaining flow; do not create a fake “real” publication record.
7. **Confirm**: after an actual manual publication, enter its actual URL and timestamp. Mark as Published preserves the text/draft/topic snapshot and prevents duplicates. Create revision starts a new unapproved draft.
8. **Measure**: in Performance, enter actual cumulative metrics and measurement time. Leave unavailable values blank. Inspect total/rate, post and topic rollups, coverage and measurement history. A later snapshot replaces prior totals instead of adding them twice.
9. **Learn**: compare topics at consistent ages, investigate qualified replies and product clicks, and choose the next editorial experiment. Ordinary interactions are not automatically qualified engagement; account-level follower growth is not post attribution.

Before there are publications, demonstrate the honest empty state. The automated growth browser script exercises the complete flow with test-only fixtures and intercepted external navigation; those artifacts are validation evidence, not a growth claim.
